# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please don't open a public issue for security problems.

Report privately through GitHub (**Security** tab, then **Report a vulnerability**) or email **shrijeet@vexocore.in** with the subject `Security: companybrain`. Include:

- what you found and where (file, route or commit)
- steps to reproduce or a minimal proof of concept
- the impact you think it has

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 72 hours
- Critical issues are prioritised for a fix as soon as possible, with progress updates along the way
- Credit in the fix and release notes, unless you'd rather stay anonymous

## Scope

In scope: code in this repository and its default deployment.
Out of scope: third-party services, social engineering, physical attacks and denial of service.

## Safe harbour

Good-faith research that follows this policy, avoids privacy violations and data destruction, and gives us reasonable time to fix before disclosure will not face legal action from us.
